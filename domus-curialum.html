The Domus Curialum

The Domus Curialum, also known as the House of Ministers, is the lower house of the bicameral Imperial Assembly of Bristevia.

It serves as the primary legislative body representing the Empire's 182 legislative districts and provides the principal popular chamber through which the people participate directly in the legislative government of the Imperial Sovereign of Bristevia.

I. Composition and Representation

  • Total Membership: The Domus Curialum consists of exactly 182 Ministers.
  • District Representation: Each Minister represents one specific legislative district.
  • Population Basis: Legislative districts are established principally according to population.
  • Nature of Office: Ministers are elective officials chosen through direct elections by the people of their respective districts.

II. Leadership and Governance

The Domus Curialum is presided over by the Speaker of the House, who is elected by a majority vote of all its members.

Impartiality Requirement

Upon nomination, the Speaker must abandon their political party in order to ensure impartial governance of floor sessions.

Duties of the Speaker

  • Presiding over legislative debates.
  • Maintaining order within the chamber.
  • Managing floor proceedings.
  • Tracking ministerial decrees.
  • Ensuring compliance with the constitutional rules of the House.

III. Legislative and Fiscal Powers

Revenue and Finance

All appropriation, revenue, and tariff decrees, as well as measures authorizing increases in public debt, must originate exclusively in the Domus Curialum.

Impeachment

The House of Ministers possesses the exclusive authority to initiate cases of impeachment against high-ranking officials.

Articles of Impeachment require the concurrence of one-third (1/3) of all members of the Domus Curialum.

Election of the Prime Minister

The Domus Curialum participates jointly with the Magnum Consilium in the election of the Prime Minister. The election requires a two-thirds (2/3) majority vote.

Cabinet Selection

All Imperial Cabinet Ministers must be selected from among the members of the Domus Curialum.

IV. Commission on Appointments

The Domus Curialum provides twelve of its members to the Commission on Appointments.

These members are selected according to proportional representation from registered political parties and serve alongside twelve members from the Magnum Consilium.

The Commission acts upon nominations for key officials of the Realm, including Ministers of the Crown, Ambassadors, and other officials requiring confirmation.

V. Key Facts and Procedures

Three Readings

No bill may become law unless it passes three readings on separate days within the House.

Disciplinary Authority

The House may determine its own rules of proceedings. With a two-thirds vote, it may suspend a member for up to sixty days or expel a member for disorderly conduct.

Sovereign Proposal

When the Souverion wishes to propose legislation, the proposal is referred to a member of the Domus Curialum for formal introduction.

VI. Election of Ministers

Ministers of the Domus Curialum are chosen through direct elections by the people of Bristevia.

Every registered citizen who is not otherwise disqualified by law has the right to vote for members of the Imperial Assembly.

Political Party Registration

Candidates must present their names together with their registered political party during elections.

Political parties must be registered with the Commission on Imperial Elections and must uphold the sovereignty of the Realm and the 2026 Constitution.

VII. Post-Election Validation

Supreme Tribunal Review

Following an election, the consolidated list of successful candidates is submitted to the Supreme Tribunal for evaluation.

Approval

Newly elected Ministers assume office only after the declaration and approval of the Supreme Tribunal, confirming that no electoral violations occurred.

Oath of Fealty

Once cleared, Ministers take a solemn oath of office before the Chief Justice, pledging to uphold the 2026 Constitution and remain loyal to the Souverion and Souveria.

VIII. Cabinet Eligibility

The 182 Ministers constitute the exclusive legislative pool from which the Imperial Cabinet may be selected.

When the Prime Minister selects a Minister for a Cabinet portfolio, such as Justice, Finance, Foreign Affairs, or another Imperial ministry, the appointment remains subject to confirmation by the Commission on Appointments.
