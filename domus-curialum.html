THE DOMUS CURIALUM

The Domus Curialum, also known as the House of Ministers, is the lower house of the bicameral Imperial Assembly of Bristevia.

It serves as the primary legislative body representing the Empire's 182 legislative districts. Ministers are elected directly by the people of their respective districts and exercise legislative, fiscal, and constitutional responsibilities within the Imperial Government.

I. COMPOSITION AND REPRESENTATION

  • Total Membership: The Domus Curialum consists of exactly 182 Ministers.
  • District Representation: Each Minister represents one specific legislative district.
  • Population Basis: Legislative districts are established on a population basis.
  • Nature of Office: Ministers are elective officials chosen through direct elections by the people of their respective districts.
  • District Creation: The Imperial Assembly possesses the authority to create or modify legislative districts according to constitutional requirements.

II. LEADERSHIP AND GOVERNANCE

The Domus Curialum is presided over by the Speaker of the House, who is elected by a majority vote of all members.

Impartiality of the Speaker

Upon nomination for the Speakership, the Speaker-designate must abandon their political party in order to ensure impartial governance of floor proceedings and legislative debates.

Duties of the Speaker

  • Preside over legislative debates.
  • Maintain order during proceedings.
  • Recognize members during floor debates.
  • Track legislative and ministerial decrees.
  • Enforce the Rules of the Domus Curialum.

III. LEGISLATIVE AND FISCAL POWERS

Revenue and Finance

The Domus Curialum possesses exclusive authority to originate appropriation, revenue, and tariff decrees, as well as measures authorizing increases in public debt.

Legislative Readings

No bill may become law unless it passes three readings on separate days within the House.

Impeachment

The Domus Curialum possesses the exclusive power to initiate cases of impeachment against high-ranking officials of the Imperial Government.

Articles of Impeachment require the concurrence of at least one-third (1/3) of all members of the House.

Election of the Prime Minister

The Domus Curialum participates with the Magnum Consilium in a joint session for the election of the Prime Minister from among the members of either House.

Election requires a two-thirds (2/3) majority of the Imperial Assembly.

Cabinet Eligibility

Members of the Domus Curialum constitute the exclusive legislative pool from which Imperial Cabinet Ministers may be selected.

IV. COMMISSION ON APPOINTMENTS

The Domus Curialum provides twelve of its members to the Commission on Appointments.

  • Twelve Ministers are selected from the Domus Curialum.
  • Selection is based upon proportional representation of registered political parties.
  • They serve together with twelve Consuls from the Magnum Consilium.
  • The Commission confirms key officials of the Realm.

V. ELECTION OF MINISTERS

Direct Election

Ministers of the Domus Curialum are chosen through direct elections by the people. Every registered citizen who is not otherwise disqualified by law may participate in the election of Ministers.

Political Party Presentation

During elections, every candidate presents their name together with their registered political party.

Political parties must be registered with the Commission on Imperial Elections and must uphold the sovereignty of Bristevia and the 2026 Constitution.

Post-Election Validation

Following an election, the consolidated list of successful candidates is submitted to the Supreme Tribunal for review.

Newly elected Ministers may assume office only after the Supreme Tribunal has declared and approved the results.

Oath of Fealty

Upon judicial clearance, every Minister must take the solemn oath of office before the Chief Justice, pledging to uphold the 2026 Constitution and remain loyal to the Souverion and Souveria.

VI. ACCOUNTABILITY AND DISCIPLINE

  • The House determines its own rules of proceedings.
  • The House may discipline members for disorderly conduct.
  • A two-thirds (2/3) vote may suspend a member for up to 60 days.
  • A two-thirds (2/3) vote may expel a member from the House.
  • No Minister may simultaneously hold multiple elected legislative positions.

VII. SOVEREIGN'S LEGISLATIVE PROPOSAL

When the Souverion wishes to propose legislation, the proposed measure is referred to a member of the Domus Curialum for formal introduction into the legislative process.

The measure thereafter proceeds through the constitutional legislative procedure of the Imperial Assembly.

IX. CONSTITUTIONAL POSITION

The Domus Curialum forms one of the two chambers of the bicameral Imperial Assembly of Bristevia. Its principal constitutional function is to provide direct district-based representation while exercising legislative, fiscal, and impeachment-initiating powers.

Together with the Magnum Consilium, the House forms the legislative authority of the Imperial Sovereign of Bristevia, subject to the Constitution and the authority of the Sovereign.
