Domus Curialum — House of Ministers

The Domus Curialum, also known as the House of Ministers, is the lower house of the bicameral Imperial Assembly of Bristevia.

It serves as the primary legislative body representing the Empire's legislative districts. The House consists of 182 elected Ministers, with each Minister representing one district.

Ministers are chosen through direct elections by the people of their respective districts. The House is presided over by the Speaker, currently Winston Churchill, Minister for the 3rd District of Stephara.

Legislative and Fiscal Powers

  • Appropriation, revenue, tariff, and public-debt measures must originate in the Domus Curialum.
  • The House has the exclusive power to initiate impeachment proceedings against high-ranking officials.
  • Articles of Impeachment require the vote of one-third (1/3) of all Ministers.
  • The Domus Curialum participates with the Magnum Consilium in the election of the Prime Minister.
  • Imperial Cabinet Ministers are selected from the membership of the Domus Curialum.

Commission on Appointments

Twelve members of the Domus Curialum are elected to the Commission on Appointments through proportional representation from registered political parties.

They serve together with twelve members from the Magnum Consilium in confirming key officials of the Realm.

Rules of the House

  • No bill may become law unless it passes three readings on separate days within the House.
  • The House determines its own rules.
  • With a two-thirds vote, the House may suspend a Minister for up to 60 days or expel a member for disorderly conduct.
  • If the Souverion wishes to propose legislation, the proposal is referred to a member of the Domus Curialum for formal introduction.

Election and Assumption of Office

District Representation

The Domus Curialum consists of exactly 182 Ministers. Each Minister represents one legislative district.

Direct Election

Ministers are elected directly by registered citizens who are qualified to vote under Bristevian law.

Political Parties

Candidates appear on the ballot together with their registered political party. Recognized parties include the Conservative, Liberal, Progressive Alliance, Green, Republican, Democratic, Labor, National Unity, Centrist, Socialist, Communist, and other constitutionally recognized parties.

Supreme Tribunal Review

Following the election, the consolidated list of winners is submitted to the Supreme Tribunal for review before newly elected Ministers formally assume office.

Oath of Fealty

After clearance by the Supreme Tribunal, Ministers take their oath of office before the Chief Justice, pledging to uphold the 2026 Constitution and remain loyal to the Souverion and Souveria.
