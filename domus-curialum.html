THE DOMUS CURIALUM

House of Ministers · Lower House of the Imperial Assembly

The Domus Curialum, also known as the House of Ministers, is the lower house of the bicameral Imperial Assembly of Bristevia. It serves as the principal legislative chamber representing the Empire's legislative districts and is composed of elected Ministers chosen directly by the people.

182 MINISTERS
30 PROVINCES
3 REQUIRED READINGS
⅔ DISCIPLINARY VOTE

PRESIDING OFFICER

The Domus Curialum is presided over by the Speaker of the House, elected by a majority of all Ministers.

POWERS AND FUNCTIONS

Constitutional authority of the Domus Curialum

LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY

The Domus Curialum serves as the principal legislative chamber of the Imperial Assembly. Every bill must pass three readings on separate days before becoming law.

FISCAL AUTHORITY

Appropriation, revenue, tariff and measures authorizing increases in public debt must originate exclusively in the Domus Curialum.

IMPEACHMENT INITIATION

The House possesses the exclusive authority to initiate impeachment proceedings against high officials. Articles of Impeachment require the concurrence of one-third of all members.

PRIME MINISTER ELECTION

The Domus Curialum participates with the Magnum Consilium in joint session for the election of the Prime Minister, requiring a two-thirds majority.

CABINET ELIGIBILITY

Members of the Imperial Cabinet are selected exclusively from the membership of the Domus Curialum and are subject to the applicable confirmation requirements.

COMMISSION ON APPOINTMENTS

Twelve Ministers are selected through proportional party representation to serve with twelve Consuls on the Commission on Appointments.

DISCIPLINARY AUTHORITY

The House determines its own rules and may suspend a member for up to sixty days or expel a member upon the constitutionally required two-thirds vote.

SOVEREIGN PROPOSALS

When the Souverion wishes to propose legislation, the proposal is formally introduced through a member of the Domus Curialum.

ELECTION AND ASSUMPTION OF OFFICE

Ministers are elected directly by the people of their respective legislative districts. Candidates appear on the ballot together with their registered political-party affiliation. Following an election, the consolidated roster of elected Ministers is submitted to the Supreme Tribunal for review and validation before the successful candidates formally assume office. Once cleared, each Minister takes the constitutional oath of office before the Chief Justice, pledging loyalty to the Souverion and Souveria and adherence to the 2026 Constitution.

OFFICIAL ROSTER NOTE

The official constitutional membership of the Domus Curialum is 182 Ministers. The supplied election records contain three additional incomplete administrative entries: two recorded districts for Basilion and one unnamed district for Aurelthia. These incomplete records are not counted in the official 182-member roster displayed above.
