CONSTITUTIONAL FUNCTIONS

Composition and Representation

The Domus Curialum, also known as the House of Ministers, consists of exactly 182 elected members. Each Minister represents one legislative district of the Imperial Sovereign of Bristevia.

Ministers are elected directly by the people of their respective districts. The districts are established according to population and may be created or modified by the Imperial Assembly.

Leadership — The Speaker

The Domus Curialum is presided over by the Speaker of the House, who is elected by a majority vote of all members.

The current Speaker is Winston Churchill, Minister for the 3rd District of Stephara.

Upon nomination, the Speaker must abandon their political party in order to maintain impartiality in the administration of the House.

Legislative and Fiscal Powers

  • All appropriation, revenue, and tariff decrees must originate in the Domus Curialum.
  • Measures authorizing increases in public debt must originate in the House.
  • The House possesses the exclusive power to initiate impeachment proceedings.
  • Articles of Impeachment require a one-third vote of all members.
  • The House participates jointly with the Magnum Consilium in the election of the Prime Minister.
  • Imperial Cabinet Ministers must be chosen from the members of the Domus Curialum.

Commission on Appointments

Twelve members of the Domus Curialum serve on the Commission on Appointments. These members are selected through proportional representation among registered political parties.

They serve alongside twelve Consuls from the Magnum Consilium in reviewing and confirming key appointments of the Realm.

Legislative Procedure and Discipline

  • Bills must pass three readings on separate days.
  • The House may establish its own rules of proceedings.
  • A two-thirds vote may suspend a member for up to 60 days.
  • A two-thirds vote may expel a member for disorderly conduct.
  • A proposal of the Souverion may be formally introduced through a member of the Domus Curialum.
