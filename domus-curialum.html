THE DOMUS CURIALUM

The Domus Curialum, also known as the House of Ministers, is the lower house of the bicameral Imperial Assembly of Bristevia. It represents the legislative districts of the Empire and consists of exactly 182 elected Ministers.

Each Minister represents one district and is chosen by direct election by the people. The House is presided over by the Speaker of the House.

LEGISLATIVE AND FISCAL POWERS

  • Appropriation, revenue, tariff, and public-debt measures must originate in the Domus Curialum.
  • The House possesses the exclusive power to initiate impeachment proceedings.
  • Articles of Impeachment require the vote of one-third (1/3) of all Ministers.
  • The House participates jointly with the Magnum Consilium in the election of the Prime Minister.
  • Imperial Cabinet Ministers are selected from the membership of the Domus Curialum.

SPEAKER OF THE HOUSE

The Domus Curialum is presided over by the Speaker of the House, who is elected by a majority vote of all Ministers.

Winston Churchill currently serves as Speaker of the House while representing the 3rd District of Stephara.

Upon nomination as Speaker, the Speaker abandons political-party affiliation for the purpose of ensuring impartial administration of the House.

ELECTION AND ASSUMPTION OF OFFICE

Direct Election

Ministers are chosen through direct elections by the qualified citizens of their respective districts.

Political Parties

Candidates appear together with their registered political party. Recognized political organizations include Conservative, Liberal, Progressive Alliance, Green, Republican, Democratic, Labor, National Unity, Centrist, Socialist, Communist, Radical, and other constitutionally recognized parties.

Supreme Tribunal Review

Following an election, the consolidated list of winners is submitted to the Supreme Tribunal for evaluation before newly elected Ministers formally assume office.

Oath of Fealty

Upon clearance, Ministers take their oath of office before the Chief Justice, swearing to uphold the 2026 Constitution and remain loyal to the Souverion and Souveria.

RULES OF THE HOUSE

  • No bill may become law unless it passes three readings on separate days within the House.
  • The Domus Curialum has authority to determine its own rules.
  • With a two-thirds vote, the House may suspend a Minister for up to sixty days.
  • The House may expel a member for disorderly conduct by the constitutionally required vote.
  • When the Souverion proposes legislation, the proposal is referred to a member of the Domus Curialum for formal introduction.
