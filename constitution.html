THE 2026 CONSTITUTION OF THE
IMPERIAL SOVEREIGN OF BRISTEVIA

UNDER THE ETERNAL CROWN

PREAMBLE

By the grace of the Eternal Crown, under the dominion of His Majesty the Souverion and Her Majesty the Souveria, We, the sovereign people of Bristevia, in unity and loyalty, establish this Constitution as the foundation of our eternal Empire guided by order, wisdom, and sovereign will to secure justice, uphold honor, maintain peace, and safeguard the everlasting authority of the Imperial Sovereign. Thus, in the name of Bristevia and under Heaven’s light, we ordain and promulgate this Constitution.

ARTICLE I — THE IMPERIAL SOVEREIGN OF BRISTEVIA

Bristevia is a sovereign and eternal monarchy, composed of provinces and the capital city of Stephara.
Its government shall be known as the Imperial Sovereign of Bristevia (ISB), exercising authority under the divine right and constitutional rule of the Souverion.
Sovereignty resides in the Souverion and the Bristevian People, whose allegiance is pledged to the Eternal Crown.

ARTICLE II — DECLARATION OF PRINCIPLES AND IMPERIAL POLICIES

Section 1. The State shall embody the ideals of loyalty, justice, and unity under the benevolent reign of the Souverion.
Section 2. The authority of the Souverion is absolute and indivisible, yet bound by the Constitution. His decrees must conform to the law of the realm.
Section 3. The Souverion and the Constitution stand as equal pillars of sovereignty. No amendment may diminish the supremacy of the Souverion.
Section 4. The government shall promote the welfare of all Bristevians and preserve harmony among its provinces.
Section 5. The Imperial Sovereign shall pursue peace and friendship with other realms, guided by honor and mutual respect.
Section 6. Separation of powers and checks and balances must be observed.
Section 7. The Constitution is the highest law, and all laws must be bound by this Constitution.
Section 8. The separation of the Church and the State shall be inviolable.

ARTICLE III — THE IMPERIAL BILL OF RIGHTS

Section 1. All Bristevians are entitled to liberty, dignity, and justice.
Section 2. No person shall be deprived of life, liberty, or property without due process of law, nor denied equal protection under the laws of the realm.
Section 3. Freedom of thought, belief, expression, and peaceful assembly shall be respected, provided they do not threaten the unity of the Crown or the security of Bristevia.
Section 4. The death penalty may be imposed, unless for compelling reasons involving heinous crimes, the Imperial Assembly hereafter provides for its prohibition or limitation.

ARTICLE IV — CITIZENSHIP

Section 1. Citizenship shall be defined by Imperial Law.
Section 2. Natural-born Bristevians are those whose father or mother is a subject of Bristevia.
Section 3. Citizenship may be acquired or lost in accordance with laws enacted by the Imperial Assembly.
Section 4. Each Province shall determine its list of citizens and must provide the exact population count during the census.
Section 5. Registration of citizenship and related affairs shall be conducted by the Ministry assigned by law.

ARTICLE V — SUFFRAGE

Section 1. Suffrage shall be exercised by all citizens of Bristevia not otherwise disqualified by law.
Section 2. Voting may be conducted manually or by secure means prescribed by the Electoral Code.
Section 3. The Prime Minister shall be chosen jointly by both the Magnum Consilium and the Domus Curialum, voting as one Imperial Assembly. The Prime Minister shall be nominated from among the Members of either House and must secure the votes of two-thirds (2/3) of all Members of both Houses.
Section 4. The Speaker of the Domus Curialum shall preside over its sessions and must secure a majority vote of all its Members.
Section 5. The Chairman, who shall preside over the Magnum Consilium, must likewise secure a majority vote of all its Members.
Section 6. The presiders of both Houses must abandon their political parties before being nominated to ensure impartiality.
Section 7. Registered citizens shall have the right to vote for Members of the Imperial Assembly and local government officials.
Section 8. No candidate shall be elected to more than one position at the same time.
Section 9. After the election and before assuming office, the consolidated list of winners must be submitted to the Supreme Tribunal (ST) for evaluation and shall immediately assume office upon declaration and approval if no violation is found. Exemptions to this provision are the Prime Minister, the Speaker, and the Chairman.
Section 10. During the election, each candidate must present their name along with their political party. Example: Name (Party).

ARTICLE VI — THE LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT

Section 1. The legislative power shall be vested in a bicameral Imperial Assembly of Bristevia, consisting of the Magnum Consilium (Upper House of Consuls) and the Domus Curialum (Lower House of Ministers).
Section 2. The Magnum Consilium shall be composed of one Member from each province, representing provincial interests. The number of seats may increase if new provinces are created by law.
Section 3. The Domus Curialum shall consist of one Member representing each district.
Section 4. The Prime Minister, elected jointly by both Houses as provided in Article V, shall head the government and preside over its administration.
Section 5. The Speaker of the Domus Curialum shall preside over its sessions. The Chairman shall preside over the Magnum Consilium.
Section 6. Laws shall be initiated exclusively by the Imperial Assembly. If the Souverion desires to propose a law, He shall refer it to a Member of the Domus Curialum for introduction in proper form.

LAW-MAKING PROCEDURES

Section 7. Every bill passed by the Imperial Assembly shall embrace only one subject, which shall be expressed in the title.
Section 8. No bill shall become law unless it has passed three readings on separate days. Printed copies in final form must be distributed to Members at least three days before passage, except when the Souverion certifies the bill as urgent.
Section 9. Upon the last reading, no amendment shall be allowed, and the vote shall be taken immediately and recorded.
Section 10. Every bill passed by both Houses shall, before becoming law, be presented to the Souverion. If He approves, He shall sign it; otherwise, He shall veto it and return it with objections. If, after reconsideration, two-thirds (2/3) of both Houses approve it, the bill shall become law. The Souverion may veto specific items in appropriation, revenue, or tariff bills without affecting the remainder.

READINGS OF BILLS

Section 11(a). First Reading – The bill shall be introduced by the Speaker or Chairman, stating its number, title, and author, and referred to the proper committee.
Section 11(b). Committee Action – The committee shall conduct hearings, propose amendments, and prepare a report, decided upon by at least ten percent (10%) of the Members of the House. Committee seats shall reflect proportional representation from political parties and organizations registered within the Imperial Realm.
Section 11(c). Second Reading – Debate and amendment by the House.
Section 11(d). Third Reading – Final vote and tally of all Members.
Section 11(e). Transmission – The approved bill shall be sent to the other House for concurrence, where it shall undergo the same process.
Section 11(f). Conference Committee – A joint committee shall reconcile differing versions of the bill.
Section 11(g). Enrollment and Authentication – The final bill shall be prepared and transmitted to the Souverion for approval.

FORMAT OF BILLS AND LAWS

Section 12. Bill Format:
HB Number – Title
Constitutional Basis (Article and Section only)
Proposed Provision
Author’s Name
Souverion’s Action: The Souverion may approve, veto, or return the bill.
Section 13. Law Format:
SA Number
Constitutional Basis (Article and Section only)
Proposed Provision
Author’s Name
After approval, the law shall be numbered as a Sovereign Act (SA) and recorded sequentially (e.g., SA1, SA2, etc.).
Section 14. The Magnum Consilium, the Domus Curialum, or any of their respective committees may conduct inquiries in aid of legislation.
Section 15. No amendment may diminish the sovereignty of the Souverion or the authority of His Crown.
Section 16. Only political parties registered under Section 16(b) shall be free to organize, provided they uphold the sovereignty of the Realm and the Constitution.
(a) Political parties shall register with the Commission on Imperial Elections, which shall regulate their organization, finances, and participation in elections.
(b) Recognized political parties shall include: Green Party, Liberal Party, Democratic Party, Republican Party, National Unity, Progressive Alliance, Socialist Party, Communist Party, Centrist, Labor, Conservative, Radical, and Independent.
Section 17. Each House of the Imperial Assembly may determine the rules of its proceedings, discipline its Members for disorderly conduct, and, with the concurrence of two-thirds (2/3) of all Members, suspend or expel a Member. The penalty of suspension shall not exceed sixty (60) days.
Section 18. Commission on Appointments. There shall be a Commission on Appointments, composed of the Chairman of the Magnum Consilium as ex officio Chairman, twelve Members of the Magnum Consilium, and twelve Members of the Domus Curialum, elected by their respective Houses based on proportional representation from registered political parties. The Chairman shall not vote except in the event of a tie. The Commission shall act upon all nominations and appointments within thirty (30) session days and decide by majority vote of all its Members.
Section 19. All appropriation, revenue, or tariff decrees, as well as decrees authorizing increases in public debt, decrees of local application, and private decrees, shall originate exclusively in the Domus Curialum. The Magnum Consilium may propose or concur with amendments in accordance with legislative procedure.

APPROPRIATIONS AND FISCAL AUTHORITY

Section 20. No law shall authorize the transfer of appropriations; however, the Souverion, Prime Minister, Chairman of the Magnum Consilium, Speaker of the Domus Curialum, Chief Justice of the Supreme Tribunal, and Heads of Constitutional Commissions may, by Imperial Law, augment any item in the General Appropriations Decree for their respective offices from savings in other items. Discretionary funds appropriated for particular officials shall be disbursed solely for public purposes, supported by vouchers, and subject to guidelines prescribed by Imperial Law to ensure transparency and accountability.

ARTICLE VII — THE IMPERIAL EXECUTIVE

Section 1. The executive power shall be vested in the Souverion of Bristevia, the Supreme Monarch and Head of State, and the Prime Minister as Head of Government.
Section 2. The Souverion shall reign by right of succession and divine ordination, and shall be assisted by the Prime Minister and the Imperial Cabinet in matters of governance.
Section 3. The Souverion’s acts and decisions shall conform to this Constitution. His actions may be questioned before the Supreme Tribunal, but his sovereignty shall remain absolute and undiminished.
Section 4. The Prime Minister shall direct the government’s policies and implement its laws. He may be removed by a two-thirds (2/3) vote of both Houses voting jointly.
Section 5. The Souveria shall serve as Co-Sovereign and symbol of unity and grace of the Empire.
Section 6. The Imperial Cabinet of Ministries. The Imperial Cabinet shall consist of the Ministries for Justice, Finance, Defense, Foreign Affairs, Education, Health, Agriculture, Labor, Infrastructure, Social Welfare, and Provincial Administration, under the direction of the Prime Minister.
Section 7. The Prime Minister shall make executive and administrative appointments in accordance with Imperial Law.
Section 8. The Prime Minister shall have control of executive Imperial ministries, bureaus, and offices.
Section 9. Council Chamber of Advisers. The Souverion shall appoint a Council Chamber of Advisers to provide counsel on matters of state.

ARTICLE VIII — THE JUDICIARY

Section 1. The judicial power shall be vested in one Supreme Tribunal and such Special Courts and lower Regional Courts as may be established by law.
Section 2. The Supreme Tribunal shall be composed of eight (8) Justices and one (1) Chief Justice, all appointed by the Souverion without a fixed term.
Section 3. Justices shall hold a permanent seat and are prohibited from being nominated or appointed to any other position.
Section 4. The judicial power includes the authority to settle controversies involving rights and to determine whether any act of government conforms to this Constitution.
Section 5. Powers and Jurisdiction of the Supreme Tribunal. The Supreme Tribunal shall exercise original and appellate jurisdiction as provided by this Constitution and Imperial Law.
Section 6. Stare Decisis. Decisions of the Supreme Tribunal applying or interpreting the laws or the Constitution of the Imperial Sovereign of Bristevia shall form part of the legal system of Bristevia.
Section 7. There shall be Special Courts of Appeal for Administrative, Family, Criminal, Civil, Commercial, and Military matters.
Section 8. The Rules of Court shall be enacted by the Supreme Tribunal.
Section 9. The Supreme Tribunal may repeal or nullify any law found to be unconstitutional.
Section 10. The Executive and Legislative branches are bound by the decisions of the Judiciary.
Section 11. Regional Courts shall serve as courts of first instance.
Section 12. The Supreme Tribunal shall have administrative supervision over all courts and personnel thereof.
Section 13. Every Region must consist of at least one Regional Court.

ARTICLE IX — LOCAL GOVERNMENT ADMINISTRATION

Section 1. The territorial and political subdivisions of Bristevia are Provinces, Cities, Towns, and Villages.
Section 2. A Governor shall lead each Province, and a Mayor shall govern each City or Town.
Section 3. Local government shall be organized into National, Provincial, City or Town, and Village Governments.
Section 4. The National Government shall consist of the Souverion, Prime Minister, Imperial Cabinet, Imperial Assembly, Supreme Tribunal, and national ministries and agencies.
Section 5. Districts exist for representation in the House of Ministers.
Section 6. The Village shall be the lowest local subdivision.
Section 7. The creation of Provinces, Districts, Cities, Towns, or Villages shall be determined exclusively by the Imperial Assembly.
Section 8. A Region shall consist of no fewer than two Provinces.
Section 9. The Imperial Assembly shall enact a Local Government Code.
Section 10. The Prime Minister shall exercise general supervision over local governments.
Section 11. Local Government Units may associate, consolidate, or coordinate their efforts in accordance with Imperial Law.

ARTICLE X — NATIONAL DEFENSE AND SECURITY

Section 1. The defense of the Realm shall rest upon the Imperial Armed Forces of Bristevia.
Section 2. The Souverion is the Commander-in-Chief of all armed forces.
Section 3. The Minister of Defense shall administer national defense and internal security.
Section 4. The Imperial Assembly shall have the power to declare war, appropriate defense funds, and ratify defense treaties.
Section 5. Military service may be voluntary or compulsory as provided by law.
Section 6. The armed forces shall remain non-partisan.
Section 7. Martial Authority and the Privilege of the Writ. The constitutional limitations governing Martial Rule and suspension of the privilege of the Writ shall be observed.

ARTICLE XI — FINANCE AND TAXATION

Section 1. The power to tax and appropriate funds shall reside in the Domus Curialum, with the concurrence of the Magnum Consilium and subject to the assent of the Souverion.
Section 2. No tax or expenditure shall be imposed or made except by law.
Section 3. All revenues shall be held in the Imperial Treasury.
Section 4. The Minister of Finance shall manage the Imperial Treasury.
Section 5. The Imperial Auditor-General shall examine all accounts of the Realm.
Section 6. Taxation shall be uniform and equitable and shall maintain a progressive system.
Section 7. Public funds shall be used solely for authorized public purposes.
Section 8. Emergency fiscal powers may be exercised as provided by Constitution and law.

ARTICLE XII — ACCOUNTABILITY OF PUBLIC OFFICERS

Section 1. Public office is a trust.
Section 2. Public officers shall be accountable for their actions.
Section 3. The Domus Curialum shall investigate acts and conduct of public officials in aid of legislation.
Section 4. The Supreme Tribunal shall have jurisdiction over cases involving misconduct, impeachment, or suspension of high officials.
Section 5. The law shall provide for declaration of assets, liabilities, and interests.
Section 6. No person shall hold more than one public office at a time, except for the Co-Sovereign.
Section 7. Royal Clemency and Amnesty. The Souverion may grant reprieves, commutations, pardons, remissions, and amnesty as provided by this Constitution and Imperial Law.

ARTICLE XIII — THE CONSTITUTIONAL COMMISSIONS

The Constitutional Commissions shall be independent and co-equal bodies consisting of the Imperial Civil Service Commission, Imperial Commission on Elections, and Imperial Commission on Audit.
Their powers, appointments, tenure, fiscal autonomy, and duties shall be governed by this Constitution and Imperial Law.

ARTICLE XIV — THE FAMILY

Section 1. The family is the foundation of the Bristevian Realm and shall be protected by the State.
Section 2. Marriage, as a sacred union, is recognized as the moral basis of society.
Section 3. The State recognizes the sanctity of family life.
Section 4. Parents shall have the primary right and duty to care for and educate their children.
Section 5. The State shall equally protect the life of the mother and the life of the unborn from conception.

ARTICLE XV — EDUCATION, SCIENCE, CULTURE, AND NATIONAL DEVELOPMENT

Section 1. The State shall promote education, science, culture, and national defense.
Section 2. The Souverion shall provide guidance and strategic oversight over national development programs.
Section 3. All citizens shall have the right to access quality education and opportunities for cultural, scientific, and technological advancement.

ARTICLE XVI — AMENDMENTS OR REVISIONS

Section 1. Amendments may be proposed by a three-fourths (3/4) vote of all Members of the Imperial Assembly or by a Constitutional Assembly convened by Imperial Decree.

ARTICLE XVII — ACCOUNTABILITY AND IMPEACHMENT

Section 1. The House of Ministers shall have the exclusive power to initiate impeachment cases.
Section 2. The Magnum Consilium shall have the sole authority to try and decide impeachment cases.
Section 3. Impeachable officers include the Prime Minister, Members of the Supreme Tribunal, Members of the Imperial Assembly, Ministers, presiding officers, Governors, Mayors, appointed officials, and Members of the Constitutional Commissions.
Section 4. Grounds include treason, corruption, abuse of power, constitutional violation, dereliction of duty, and betrayal of public trust.
Section 5. A verified complaint may be filed and referred to the proper Committee.
Section 6. One-third (1/3) of all Members of the House of Ministers shall be required to approve Articles of Impeachment.
Section 7. The Magnum Consilium shall try impeachment cases.
Section 8. Judgment shall extend to removal and disqualification from office.
Section 9. No impeachment proceeding shall be initiated against the same official more than once within a year.
Section 10. The Imperial Assembly shall promulgate rules on impeachment.
Section 11. There shall be an independent Office of the Imperial Sentinel.
Section 12. The Office of the Special Prosecutor shall continue to function as provided by law.
Section 13. The Imperial Sentinel shall investigate unlawful, unjust, or improper acts of public officials.
Section 14. The Office of the Imperial Sentinel shall enjoy fiscal autonomy.
Section 15. The Empire may recover properties unlawfully acquired by public officials.
Section 16. Certain public officials shall be prohibited from receiving loans or financial accommodation from state-owned financial institutions during tenure.
Section 17. Public officers shall submit declarations of assets, liabilities, and net worth.
Section 18. Public officers shall owe allegiance to the Imperial Sovereign of Bristevia.

ARTICLE XVIII — APPOINTMENTS AND ELECTIVE OFFICES

Division A — Appointive Officials
Section 1. The power to appoint officials shall be vested in the Prime Minister and such authorities as may be designated by law.
Section 2. Appointments shall be based upon merit, integrity, fitness, competence, and moral character.
Section 3. Ministers, agency heads, ambassadors, judges, board members, and other non-elective officers shall be filled by appointment.
Section 4. Appointments shall be subject to confirmation as provided by law.
Section 5. The Imperial Assembly shall exercise legislative oversight over appointments.
Division B — Elective Officials
Section 6. Elective offices shall not be filled through appointment.

ARTICLE XIX — TERRITORY

Section 1. The national territory of the Imperial Sovereign of Bristevia shall consist of all lands, islands, waters, and airspace belonging to the Realm.
Section 2. The Realm shall be divided into Provinces, Districts, Cities, Towns, Villages, and Regions.
Section 3. Autonomous Regions may be established by law.
Section 4. Territorial boundaries shall be fixed and published in the official Bristevia Atlas and Map.
Section 5. All territorial units remain subject to the national authority for purposes of defense and security.
Section 6. Provincial Governors, Mayors, and Village Chiefs shall exercise authority within their respective territories.

ARTICLE XX — GENERAL PROVISIONS

Section 1. Supremacy of the Constitution. This Constitution is the supreme law of the Imperial Sovereign of Bristevia.
Section 2. Oaths of Office. All public officials shall take an oath to uphold and defend the Constitution.
Section 3. Official Language. The official language shall be established by law.
Section 4. National Symbols. The flag, anthem, seal, Royal Emblem, Royal Symbol, and other symbols of the Imperial Sovereign of Bristevia shall be established by law.
