Imperium Realm Court Decree

Imperial Proclamation

Let it be known throughout the realm that Bristevia stands as a sovereign symbol of dignity, strength, wisdom, and noble governance.

By the authority of the Imperial Crown, the realm shall stand united beneath the banner of Bristevia, guided by honor and ordered by wisdom.

Pillars of the Realm

♛

Sovereignty

The Crown shall preserve the independence, dignity, and authority of the realm.

⚔

Honor

Integrity and courage shall remain the foundation of every noble undertaking.

⚖

Justice

Law shall govern the realm, and justice shall stand above privilege.

✦

Prosperity

The realm shall cultivate knowledge, civilization, and enduring prosperity.

The Imperial Court

II Imperial Council

Royal Audience
