THE PROVINCIAL REALM

The Imperial Sovereign of Bristevia is composed of exactly thirty recognized provinces, organized into five Strategic Regions. Each province is governed by an elected Governor and a Provincial Council and maintains its own historical identity within the unified administrative structure of the Empire.


Every province is represented by one Consul in the Magnum Consilium and by multiple representatives in the Domus Curialum. Provincial administration operates under the supervision of the Ministry of Provincial Administration and the Bristevia Local Government Code.

STEPHARA CITY

Imperial Capital of Bristevia

Stephara City is the capital of the Imperial Sovereign of Bristevia and the principal seat of the Crown. It stands within the historic province of Stephara and serves as the political, ceremonial and diplomatic center of the Realm.


The city became the center of Stephen II's political consolidation and later served as the seat of the independent Bristevian monarchy. During ERA 11, Stephara City hosted negotiations that contributed to the United Valterra framework.

CENTRAL REGION

The Imperial Core

PROVINCE I

STEPHARA

The Imperial Heartland
REGION Central
CAPITAL Stephara City
AREA 14,895 km²
POPULATION 3,200,000
REGIONAL CENTER Valloré City

Historical Record

Stephara is considered the historical birthplace of centralized Bristevian authority. Before becoming the imperial capital, the region consisted of settlements surrounding the Stephara Strait.

During the Valtherium era, it became the center of Stephen II's political consolidation, when Festona, Brionne and Valloré were gradually brought under the Stephara Principality.

The province later became the seat of the independent Bristevian monarchy under Stephen X following the collapse of Valtherium influence. The Battle of Stephara Fields is traditionally remembered as the first major victory of the independent Bristevian army.

During ERA 11, Stephara City hosted negotiations that contributed to the United Valterra framework. Under Stephen XXVI, the province retained its position as the political and ceremonial heart of the Empire.

Historical Importance: Birthplace of the Stephara Principality, capital of Bristevia, seat of the Souverion and principal diplomatic center.
PROVINCE II

JAYBRIENNE

The Inland Breadbasket
REGION Central
CAPITAL Valloré City
AREA 18,342 km²
POPULATION 2,400,000
ROLE Agricultural Core

Historical Record

Jaybrienne developed from ancient Brionne agricultural settlements organized around fertile river valleys and inland plains under Valtherium influence.

Stephen II's unification brought Jaybrienne into the Stephara political sphere. Its agricultural production became essential to maintaining Bristevia's armies.

During the wars of ERA 7 and ERA 8, Jaybrienne supplied grain, livestock and recruits. The Granary Roads, constructed during Stephen XIV's administrative reforms, connected the province with Stephara.

During ERA 11, Jaybrienne supplied food and refugee relief materials during the continental crisis.

Historical Importance: Agricultural foundation of Bristevian state power.

WEST REGION

The Industrial Powerhouse

PROVINCE III

ORLENCIA

The Fallen Kingdom
REGION West
CAPITAL Drakemount City
AREA 16,985 km²
POPULATION 1,100,000
PALACE Obsidian Hall

Historical Record

Orlencia is unique because the province bears the name of the kingdom that Bristevia ultimately destroyed.

For centuries, Orlencia dominated western maritime trade and exercised influence over Festona and Brionne. Its coastal aristocracy controlled important ports and merchant houses.

Its decline began when Bristevia expanded westward. The decisive events were the Battle of Cindora and Battle of Lunebrook. Following Lunebrook, the Orlencian command structure collapsed and the kingdom dissolved.

The old Orlencian royal complex was transformed into Obsidian Hall, later becoming the provincial palace.

Historical Importance: Former rival kingdom and one of the foundational territories of western Bristevia.
PROVINCE IV

WESTRION

The Amber Coast
REGION West
CAPITAL Goldhaven City
AREA 17,882 km²
POPULATION 1,400,000
ROLE Industrial Hub

Historical Record

Westrion developed around western cliffs and natural harbors connecting Orlencia's maritime economy with Bristevia.

During the Orlencian period, merchant families established settlements along the Ambercoast Cliffs. After the fall of Orlencia, these communities became important intermediaries between former Orlencian territories and Bristevian authorities.

During Stephen XV's consolidation, Westrion became one of the first western territories to adopt standardized Bristevian taxation.

The province later industrialized in metalworking, ship components and coastal manufacturing.

PROVINCE V

COASTRIA

The Maritime Scholar
REGION West
CAPITAL Seamaris City
AREA 19,560 km²
POPULATION 900,000
ROLE Naval Science

Historical Record

Coastria began as fishing and shipbuilding settlements along the western sea.

After the absorption of Orlencia, Bristevian commanders recognized the need for a permanent center of naval research.

The Coastrian Maritime School became famous for navigation, naval engineering, cartography and maritime law.

During ERA 10, Coastrian officers participated in naval reforms following the Battle of Bristevia Gulf.

Historical Importance: Scientific and intellectual center of Bristevian naval power.
PROVINCE VI

RONJIEL

The Highland Terraces
REGION West
CAPITAL Ronjiel City
AREA 19,276 km²
POPULATION 1,300,000
PALACE Jaymount Hall

Historical Record

Ronjiel originated as a highland farming society that maintained considerable independence from coastal kingdoms.

Its famous Jaymount Highlands developed through centuries of terraced agriculture.

Stephen XIV's administrative reforms brought permanent royal officials into the region.

The province later became an important recruitment center for mountain infantry.

PROVINCE VII

VIRELION

The Province of Seven Rivers
REGION West
CAPITAL Thalindor City
AREA 20,212 km²
POPULATION 2,100,000
WATERS Seven Rivers

Historical Record

Virelion grew around seven major river systems connecting western inland settlements with the coast.

Before centralization, the region was divided among competing river communities. Local tradition attributes the first permanent river compact to Lord Virel.

Following the absorption of Orlencia, Bristevia used Virelion as an inland transportation corridor.

Its rivers became particularly important for transporting grain, weapons and troops during military mobilizations.

PROVINCE VIII

DRAXENOR

The Wetland Seat
REGION West
CAPITAL Shadowfen City
AREA 20,210 km²
POPULATION 900,000
LANDSCAPE Wetlands

Historical Record

Draxenor developed in extensive wetlands and marshes. Ancient settlements were built on raised ground and wooden foundations.

After incorporation into Bristevia, engineers drained portions of the wetlands and developed them for mining, manufacturing and military supply.

During the industrial era, Draxenor became a major center for heavy industry.

Its people developed a strong cultural identity around the saying: “What cannot be conquered by water must be built upon it.”

PROVINCE IX

FALRION

The Western Industrial Capital
REGION West
CAPITAL Silverhold City
AREA 17,910 km²
POPULATION 2,000,000
ROLE Industry

Historical Record

Falrion began as a frontier fortress protecting western trade roads.

The fortress became Silverhold City, which expanded dramatically following Bristevian industrialization.

During the later imperial period, Falrion produced weapons, machinery and military equipment.

During the Second Pan-Continental War, Silverhold's factories supplied the Imperial Maritime Directorate.

PROVINCE X

REGALITH

The Old Stone Province
REGION West
CAPITAL Cresvane City
AREA 20,306 km²
POPULATION 1,518,000
PALACE Stonebay Hall

Historical Record

Regalith developed around ancient stone settlements predating the Bristevian monarchy.

The Crownstone Citadel was originally constructed by a pre-Bristevian mountain dynasty and later converted into a royal fortress.

During Stephen XXV's Decree of Singular Sovereignty, several hereditary noble houses were formally stripped of regional governing authority.

Historical Importance: A major site in the transition from the old aristocratic order to centralized imperial administration.

NORTH REGION

The Defensive Belt

PROVINCE XI

NORTHAVEN

The Northern Shield
REGION North
CAPITAL Frostwall City
AREA 29,985 km²
POPULATION 800,000
REGIONAL CENTER Jaeyara City

Historical Record

Northaven has always been a frontier province. Its communities developed around mountain passes leading toward the northern territories.

During the Frostwall War, Northaven became the main Bristevian military staging area. Frostwall Fort was expanded into a massive defensive complex.

The fall of Frostwall City during ERA 9 opened the Northavian interior to Bristevian occupation and permanently changed the strategic geography of the Empire.

Historical Importance: Symbolic guardian of the northern frontier and home of the historic Frostwall defense complex.
PROVINCE XII

MONTELUNE

The Province of High Mountains
REGION North
CAPITAL Mooncrest City
AREA 28,872 km²
POPULATION 600,000
ROLE Mountain Defense

Historical Record

Montelune was historically populated by isolated alpine communities whose valleys were difficult to reach.

During the Frostwall War, its mountain passes became crucial military routes. The Pass of Saint Lun was used by Bristevian troops to move reinforcements north.

After the war, roads and fortifications transformed Montelune into an important defensive province.

PROVINCE XIII

BASILION

The Frigid Seat
REGION North
CAPITAL Cresvane City
AREA 25,746 km²
POPULATION 700,000
MOTTO Frigid Heart

Historical Record

Basilion lies in one of the coldest parts of the northern region.

Its capital developed around a military settlement established during the early northern consolidation.

The Basilion Winter Muster became an annual military exercise designed to prepare soldiers for extreme weather.

During the Frostwall War, Basilion troops gained a reputation for operating in severe conditions.

Provincial Motto: “Frigid Heart • Enduring Strength”
PROVINCE XIV

AETHELBURG

The Stone Fortress
REGION North
CAPITAL Falcrest City
AREA 22,412 km²
POPULATION 700,000
PALACE Skyhold Palace

Historical Record

Aethelburg began as a mountain settlement surrounding a fortress.

During the Frostwall campaigns, the province supplied engineers specializing in fortifications.

Its mountain capital became one of the most dramatic cities in northern Bristevia.

Its historical culture emphasizes endurance, military discipline and mountain engineering.

PROVINCE XV

SYLVAREN

The Ancient Forest
REGION North
CAPITAL Leafmere City
AREA 22,191 km²
POPULATION 600,000
ROLE Forestry

Historical Record

Sylvaren remained heavily forested even as neighboring provinces developed.

Its ancient forests provided timber for Bristevia's early fleets and fortifications.

During the expansion of the kingdom, royal authorities established protected timber zones.

The province therefore became closely connected to forestry and naval construction.

PROVINCE XVI

JAEYARA

The Northern Spire
REGION North
CAPITAL Jaeyara City
AREA 20,217 km²
POPULATION 600,000
ROLE Regional Center

Historical Record

Jaeyara developed around a strategic mountain corridor connecting the northern provinces.

The Northern Spire Palace was constructed during the centralized imperial period as the administrative headquarters of the northern region.

During ERA 11, Jaeyara became the principal coordination point for northern military administrations.

Historical Importance: Symbol of the transformation of the northern frontier into a carefully managed imperial defense system.
PROVINCE XVII

ANDRIEL

The Northern Valley
REGION North
CAPITAL Andriel City
AREA 21,083 km²
POPULATION 688,000
ROLE Horse Breeding

Historical Record

Andriel developed in a sheltered valley between Northaven and the eastern highlands.

Unlike heavily militarized frontier provinces, Andriel became known for farming and horse breeding.

Its horses were used by Bristevian cavalry during the Frostwall War.

The Andriel Horse Roads later became civilian transportation routes connecting northern provinces with the Empire.

EAST REGION

The Trade Gateway

PROVINCE XVIII

ORINTHAL

The Highland Artisan Capital
REGION East
CAPITAL Goldspire City
AREA 14,702 km²
POPULATION 1,000,000
ROLE Artisan Center

Historical Record

Orinthal originated as mountain artisan communities.

Its craftsmen became famous for stonework, metal engraving, textiles and ceremonial objects.

After the Eldrosian-Bristevian union, Orinthal became a cultural meeting point between eastern and central traditions.

Imperial craftsmen later incorporated Orinthalian designs into government buildings throughout Bristevia.

PROVINCE XIX

EASTRIA

The Eastern Frontier
REGION East
CAPITAL Eastria City
AREA 18,890 km²
POPULATION 900,000
ROLE Trade Gateway

Historical Record

Eastria developed as a coastal frontier between established Bristevian territory and the eastern maritime world.

During the war with Caeloria, Eastria became an important staging area for Bristevian amphibious forces.

Following the establishment of the eastern imperial system, Eastria transformed from a frontier into a gateway.

PROVINCE XX

LUMINARIS

The Eastern Naval Gateway
REGION East
CAPITAL Crystalhold City
AREA 18,372 km²
POPULATION 900,000
REGIONAL CENTER Luminaris City

Historical Record

Luminaris originated from coastal territories influenced by Caeloria and other eastern maritime powers.

During ERA 10, Bristevia defeated Caelorian influence and absorbed the Luminaris territories.

Former coastal forts were converted into imperial naval stations.

During ERA 11, Luminaris became the center of Bristevia's maritime conflict and later the home of major naval facilities.

Historical Importance: Major eastern naval, navigation and maritime trade center.
PROVINCE XXI

AURELTHIA

The White-Stone Coast
REGION East
CAPITAL Moonspire City
AREA 20,785 km²
POPULATION 600,000
ROLE Architecture

Historical Record

Aurelthia developed around bright coastal plains and limestone settlements.

Its capital, Moonspire City, became famous for its white-stone architecture.

The province was historically influenced by eastern maritime cultures before gradual incorporation into Bristevian administration.

During the imperial era, Aurelthia became a center of architecture, astronomy and navigation.

PROVINCE XXII

ELDROSIA

The United Realm
REGION East
CAPITAL Raventon City
AREA 20,184 km²
POPULATION 1,200,000
LEGACY Ram–Rosé Union

Historical Record

Eldrosia was once an independent major power.

The decisive turning point came with the Ram–Rosé Union, when King Ram of Bristevia married Princess Rosé of Eldrosia.

The marriage transformed the relationship between the two states from strategic alliance into dynastic integration.

Eldrosian institutions were gradually incorporated into the Bristevian system while retaining a distinct regional identity.

Historical Importance: The province symbolizes the dynastic and political union between Eldrosia and Bristevia.
PROVINCE XXIII

GRAVENOR

The Eastern Fortresslands
REGION East
CAPITAL Stonewall City
AREA 19,306 km²
POPULATION 900,000
ROLE Fortifications

Historical Record

Gravenor originated as a chain of defensive settlements guarding inland approaches to the eastern coast.

Its fortresses were repeatedly rebuilt during periods of conflict between eastern powers.

After imperial control was established, many old forts were transformed into administrative centers.

Gravenor later became famous for military architecture and stone fortification.

PROVINCE XXIV

AQUARIS

The Island Chain
REGION East
CAPITAL Brightwater City
AREA 15,208 km²
POPULATION 1,208,000
ROLE Naval Science

Historical Record

Aquaris consists of a strategically positioned chain of islands.

Its inhabitants historically depended on fishing, navigation and maritime trade.

The islands became increasingly important after Bristevia expanded into the eastern maritime system.

Aquaris eventually developed into a major naval and navigation center.

SOUTH REGION

The Agricultural Breadbasket

PROVINCE XXV

SOUTHMERE

The Southern Gateway
REGION South
CAPITAL Briswick City
AREA 16,302 km²
POPULATION 1,800,000
REGIONAL CENTER Solara City

Historical Record

Southmere developed around coastal plains connecting the interior with the southern sea.

During the southern campaigns of James I, Southmere became a principal supply corridor toward Solara.

Its strategic importance increased following the fall of Solara City.

Today it functions as an agricultural and commercial link between the southern provinces and the imperial core.

PROVINCE XXVI

STARELLA

The Verdant Province
REGION South
CAPITAL Rosengarde City
AREA 16,000 km²
POPULATION 1,400,000
LANDMARK Verdantia Meadows

Historical Record

Starella developed around the fertile Verdantia Meadows.

For centuries, its rivers and grasslands supported pastoral communities.

Bristevian administrators recognized its agricultural potential during the expansion of the southern region.

Unlike Solara, Starella never developed a major separatist tradition and became one of the most stable southern provinces.

PROVINCE XXVII

SOLARA

The Rebellious South
REGION South
CAPITAL Solara City
AREA 16,991 km²
POPULATION 1,500,000
ROLE Agricultural Heart

Historical Record

Solara possesses one of the most complicated political histories in Bristevia.

It began as a powerful southern coastal polity and was eventually absorbed into Bristevia during southern expansion.

Its maritime aristocracy retained strong traditions of independence, eventually producing the Solara Rebellion of ERA 10.

Solara allied with Caeloria and participated in the attack that culminated in the Battle of Bristevia Gulf.

After the rebellion collapsed, Prince Emond was captured and executed. Solara ceased to exist as an autonomous political entity.

Stephen XX's government subsequently integrated the territory more directly. Under Stephen XXVI, Solara was reorganized into the South Region and part of its former territory was used to create Gabrielis.

Historical Importance: Solara represents the transition from conquered territory to rebellious polity and finally to fully integrated imperial province.
PROVINCE XXVIII

VELANDOR

The Naval Fortress
REGION South
CAPITAL Velandor City
AREA 17,434 km²
POPULATION 1,200,000
ROLE Naval Fortress

Historical Record

Velandor is a southwestern island province whose geography made it naturally defensible.

Its inhabitants developed a strong fortress culture. During the kingdom period, Velandor frequently resisted attempts by mainland nobles to interfere with local administration.

The central government eventually incorporated its defensive institutions into the Imperial Navy.

Velandor became one of the Empire's principal naval fortress zones.

PROVINCE XXIX

BRITONVALE

The Southern Crossroads
REGION South
CAPITAL Brightfield City
AREA 15,993 km²
POPULATION 895,748
ROLE Commercial Crossroads

Historical Record

Britonvale developed around roads linking southern coastal settlements with the central interior.

During the campaigns of James I, it became a major military transit zone.

Later, merchants established permanent markets along the old military roads.

The province therefore evolved from military corridor into commercial crossroads.

PROVINCE XXX

GABRIELIS

The Province of Reconciliation
REGION South
CAPITAL Gabrielis City
AREA 18,441 km²
POPULATION 1,400,000
STATUS Newest Province

Historical Record

Gabrielis is the newest of the thirty provinces.

It was created during Stephen XXVI's final administrative restructuring from former Solaran coastal districts.

The province was named after Gabriel I, remembered as an important early administrative reformer of Bristevia.

Its creation was deliberately designed as a political and administrative buffer between historically rebellious Solaran territories and the rest of the South Region.

Gabrielis therefore represents reconciliation rather than the destruction of Solara's historical identity.

Historical Importance: Gabrielis is regarded as the final major territorial punctuation mark in Bristevia's transition from fragmented territories into a unified modern state.

PROVINCIAL GOVERNANCE

Each province of Bristevia is governed by an elected Governor and a Provincial Council, which serves as the local legislative body.


Governors are elected directly by the people and must be validated by the Supreme Tribunal before assuming office. The Ministry of Provincial Administration exercises national oversight to ensure compliance with the Bristevia Local Government Code.


Every province has one Consul in the Magnum Consilium, while the Empire's 182 legislative districts provide representation in the Domus Curialum.

THE THIRTY-PROVINCE HISTORICAL ARC

The provinces of Bristevia are more than administrative divisions. Together they form a geographical record of the Empire's political development.


Stephara and Jaybrienne represent the original Bristevian core. Orlencia and the western provinces represent the absorption of the old maritime kingdoms. Northaven and the northern provinces represent the Frostwall generation and northern consolidation.


Luminaris and the eastern provinces represent the defeat of Caelorian influence and the integration of Eldrosia and the eastern maritime world.


The southern provinces preserve the history of the long southern struggle, while Gabrielis provides the final chapter: a province created from former Solaran territory as an instrument of reconciliation and administrative stability.
